# KymMaths Seed Pack & Interactive Book Format

## JSON Schema (`kymmaths-seed-pack.json`)
- `schemaVersion`: `1`
- `packId`: `"kym-maths-stage8-horizon-v1"`
- `volumes`: 5 `PatternedWorldVolume` records (`Vol I`–`Vol V`)
- `chapters`: 21 `PatternedWorldChapter` records (`Ch 0`–`Ch 20`), each including `intuitionToFormula: IntuitionToFormulaBridge`
- `explorers`: 4 Front-Matter Explorers (`x_number_tower`, `x_euclid_compass`, `x_singapore_bar_engine`, `x_bayes_calculus_bridge`)
- `atlasLessons`: 63 Spatial Atlas Lessons linked to `@saxster/atlas` chapters and figures
- `widgets`: 50 Interactive Simulations with `atlasRoute` and `intuitionBridge`
- `crossDisciplinaryBridges`: 8 STEM Bridges to Biology (`../kym_bio`), Physics (`../visualisations/atlas-of-physics`), Chemistry, and Economics
- `schoolTextbook`: Complete 16-Chapter + 3-Review TOC of Deborah Barton's *OUP Cambridge Lower Secondary Complete Mathematics 8 (Second Edition)*
- `schoolQuestions`: 80+ Student Book + 3-Tier Singapore & Checkpoint Companion Workbook items
- `diagramPlates`: 16 Apple Pencil Visual Proof & Construction Recall Plates
- `polyaStages`: 4 Pólya *How to Solve It* phases with interactive heuristic checklists & Olympiad problems
- `speedMathDecks`: 6 Mental-Math & Algebraic Fluency decks
- `visualMathTips`: 12 Visual Intuition → Formula "Aha!" cards
